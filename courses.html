Courses Offered

Programmes of Study

Three programmes in physical education — D.P.Ed at the diploma level, B.P.Ed at the bachelor's level and M.P.Ed at the master's level.

D.P.Ed B.P.Ed M.P.Ed
01

D.P.Ed — Diploma in Physical Education

2 years · After 10+2

The diploma programme in physical education — the entry route into the profession straight after Intermediate. D.P.Ed covers the foundations of sports science, health education and fitness, with practical training in games and athletics and supervised teaching practice at the school level.

Eligibility

  • Passed Intermediate (10+2)
  • Marks and sports-participation criteria as per NCTE & university norms — confirmed by the college office for the current session

Duration

  • 2 years

Where it leads

  • Physical education teacher at the elementary / primary level
  • Assistant coach and sports instructor roles
  • Progression to B.P.Ed
02

B.P.Ed — Bachelor of Physical Education

2 years · For graduates

The professional bachelor's programme in physical education. B.P.Ed trains graduates to teach physical education in schools — combining the theory of sports science, anatomy and health education with daily practical training in games, athletics and fitness, plus supervised teaching practice.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor's degree in any discipline
  • Marks and sports-participation criteria as per NCTE & university norms — confirmed by the college office for the current session

Duration

  • 2 years (4 semesters)

Where it leads

  • Physical education teacher in schools
  • Coach and fitness trainer paths
  • M.P.Ed and higher study
03

M.P.Ed — Master of Physical Education

2 years · For B.P.Ed graduates

The master's programme in physical education. M.P.Ed takes B.P.Ed graduates deeper — advanced sports science, exercise physiology, sports psychology, coaching specialisation and research methods, completed with a dissertation.

Eligibility

  • B.P.Ed degree
  • Marks criteria as per NCTE & university norms — confirmed by the college office for the current session

Duration

  • 2 years (4 semesters)

Where it leads

  • Senior physical education and coaching roles
  • Higher-education and lecturer paths, as per eligibility rules
  • Research and sports-science careers

Eligibility & norms

Admission to D.P.Ed, B.P.Ed and M.P.Ed follows NCTE and affiliating-university norms for the session. The exact eligibility criteria, seat intake and admission schedule for 2026–27 are confirmed by the college office — call before you apply.
