Programmes

Two paths in physical education

A bachelor's programme for graduates entering the profession, and a master's programme for those going deeper into coaching, sports science and research.

2 Years

B.P.Ed — Bachelor of Physical Education

The professional programme for future physical education teachers and trainers — sports science, games and athletics, health education and teaching practice.

  • For graduates — eligibility as per NCTE & university norms
  • Leads to school PE teacher, coach & trainer paths

Why GREET

Train where sport is the subject

Physical education is a profession — schools need trained PE teachers, and sport needs qualified coaches and trainers. Greet College of Physical Education trains you for both, right here at Pachrukhi, Rajdhanwar — no need to leave home for a professional degree.

  • Affiliated & recognised — programmes affiliated to Vinoba Bhave University, Hazaribag and recognised by NCTE
  • Ground + classroom — practical training in games and athletics alongside sports-science theory
  • Same trusted group — the campus and team behind GREET's teacher-training, nursing and degree colleges
  • Scholarship available — talk to the college office about what you may be eligible for
